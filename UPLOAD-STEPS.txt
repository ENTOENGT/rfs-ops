RFS CRM — Release R (To/CC follow the shipment · port list · agents by port, country or region · bulk upload · AI new enquiries)
=================================================================================================

Upload this after Release Q (and the fixed ai-agent.test.ts).

What is new
  1. Emails to customers: To and CC fill in by themselves and follow the shipment.
     - Customers -> Contacts: each contact now has "On emails: To / CC / Not by default".
     - Our people handling the shipment are copied (CC) by themselves; Settings has "Always CC on emails to customers".
     - People the customer had in To / CC on their emails, and addresses you type in, stay on that shipment's emails.
     - x next to a person takes them off that shipment's emails for good.
  2. Ports: every seaport and airport in the UN list (about 25,000) — the port boxes on the enquiry suggest as you type.
     The enquiry also has Origin country / Destination country (for "collection anywhere in Germany").
     Commercials -> Ports & regions: your regions (groups of countries) and your own ports / inland depots.
  3. Agents: Vendors -> Coverage says where each agent works (port, country or region). "Ask agents for a rate" then ticks
     every agent covering the enquiry's origin / destination, and "Email all from my Outlook" emails each one separately.
  4. Bulk upload: Administration -> Bulk upload. Download the Excel template, fill in customers (with their main salesman
     and contacts) and vendors (with their coverage), Check (row by row, nothing saved), then Import. Never duplicates.
  5. AI finds new enquiries: emails in the Salesman's / Coordinator's inbox that match no shipment are looked at by the AI.
     New enquiries (also from new customers) show first on My Outlook -> Emails to file with "Create enquiry": the AI fills
     the enquiry (and the new customer from the email signature), you check it and press Save. The same email in the
     Salesman's and the Coordinator's inbox becomes one enquiry. Emails from unknown senders that are not enquiries are not kept.
  Also: the AI box no longer offers answers that are already filled in.

  (The 25,000 ports are not in the SQL: the app loads them by itself the first time someone types in a port box.)

Step 3 — Checks
  1. Customers -> Contacts: set one contact to CC. Open an enquiry -> Email: To and CC fill in by themselves.
  2. Edit an enquiry -> Port of loading: type "hamb" -> Hamburg (DEHAM) is first. (The very first search may take a few
     seconds while the port list loads.)
  3. Vendors -> Coverage on an agent -> add a country (e.g. Germany).
  4. An enquiry with Origin country Germany -> Ask agents / carriers for a rate: that agent is ticked by itself.
  5. Administration -> Bulk upload -> Download template -> add 2 test customers -> Check -> Import.
  6. From a personal email (e.g. Gmail) send the pilot Salesman: "Please quote 1x20GP Shanghai to Jebel Ali, FOB, 8 tons
     furniture" with a company name in the signature. Within a minute it shows on My Outlook as "New enquiry?" ->
     Create enquiry -> check the form -> Create customer and open enquiry.

NEW in this one:
 - Outlook button at the top, next to "+ New", with the number of emails waiting to be filed
   (a "!" when Outlook is not connected or needs attention). Click it to open My Outlook.
 - Local charges rate cards (Commercials → Templates → Local charges): our cost for clearance, transport and handling.
   Your 6 cards are already loaded from the rate sheets (Jebel Ali, Khorfakkan, DNATA non-DG / DG, Emirates general / DG).

   shows what to check. Add them as separate lines, or inside the all-in price.
 - "If applicable" lines: shown to the customer, not counted in the total. New units: per BL / AWB, per document.
 - Vendor options: one "Offer" tick per option. And an "Own option" button on any vendor's quoted rate.
Also inside: Outlook background check, vendor options, booking reader, Track, reminders, and everything from 23 Nov.
